Displacement Blueprint

Confluence stores it.
Notion runs on it.

The sequenced route from single-team pilot to enterprise consolidation — mapped stage by stage, stakeholder by stakeholder, for the Singapore mid-market play.

Wedge → Parallel IT track → Consolidation close
01 — SEQUENCE

The plan of attack

Six stages, one non-negotiable rule: IT runs in parallel from week two, never after the pilot succeeds.
1
Week 1–2 · Discover
Qualify the wiring
Confirm seat count, trigger event, and — critically — which spaces touch Jira/Bitbucket macros or JSM. This single question decides wedge vs. full displacement.
2
Week 2–6 · Land
Single-team pilot
Migrate one real, messy Confluence space with the Champion's own team. No clean demo spaces — the pain has to be genuine for the proof to mean anything.
∥
Runs alongside stage 2 · Parallel Track
Engage IT/Security early
SSO, data residency, version-history requirements — get directional sign-off during the pilot, not after. This is the step that saves the deal.
3
Week 4 · Prove
Adoption readout
Real usage data — logins, pages created, search behaviour — plus a Champion testimonial, presented alongside the IT governance sign-off, together.
4
Week 5–6 · Expand
Adjacent team pull
Bring in a second team — HR or Sales — to demonstrate cross-functional demand before the deal reaches procurement.
5
Close
Consolidation, not replacement
Frame the CFO ask as retiring tool sprawl into one system — budget-neutral, not a rip-and-replace risk story.
02 — STAKEHOLDERS

Three rooms, three plays

Each stakeholder needs a different room to change their mind in — not the same deck three times.
Champion
Ops / CoS / L&D Lead
Resistance
Hunting across Confluence, Slack, and shadow Notion docs daily. Needs a visible quick win to build internal credibility, not another platform migration to manage.
Winning play
Knowledge Audit Workshop
90-minute live session mapping their actual tool sprawl and quantifying hours lost — in their own numbers, not yours.
Blocker
IT / Engineering Lead
Resistance
Owns the Jira relationship and migration risk. Legitimately protecting compliance certifications and governance Confluence already has.
Winning play
Security & Architecture Roundtable
Consultative session on SSO, data residency, and version retention — run in parallel, framed as due diligence, not a sales pitch.
Adjacent hook: if the team is AI-forward, demo the Notion Developer Platform (Workers/CLI/GitHub) as a complement to Jira — not a replacement pitch.
Decision Maker
CIO / COO
Resistance
Budget scrutiny is up, buying committees are larger. Wants consolidation savings, not a new line item to defend.
Winning play
Consolidation Business Case Session
POC readout bundled with the IT sign-off already secured — presented as retiring tool sprawl, not adding spend.
!

The one failure mode that kills this sequence: running the IT/Security track sequentially, after the pilot succeeds, instead of in parallel from week two. That's the difference between an 8-week close and a 6-month stall.
