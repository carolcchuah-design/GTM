Why Singapore
💡 Meet the SG AI Moment
Singapore represents Notion's highest density of digital-native talent, and a TAM of $380M and SOM of $45M anchored in fintech, tech, regional MNCs, and fast-growing digital scale ups. Singapore functions as the role model of the larger region, and can create rapid cross border adoption in shared service centers in Malaysia & Philippines, parallel financial service deployments in HK, and high growth subsidiaries in Indonesia & Vietnam.
⚡️
Why this matters now: The introduction of Custom Agents + Developer Portal coincides with a national agenda to accelerate AI transformation across Singapore enterprises. Connecting directly into regional grants and AI enablement ecosystems fast-tracks Notion as the intelligent enterprise operating system and unified knowledge backbone for APAC.

📍 Section 4 • Market Dynamics

Market Considerations

Deep-dive analysis across target territories, buying behaviors, and regional regulatory and AI environments.

💡 4 Lessons Learned

1. Each country needs its own motion.
Like Slack and Atlassian, Notion should localize the GTM motion by market — adapting buyer maps, messaging, pricing sensitivities, partners, and proof points for Singapore, Hong Kong, SEA, and India. Notion’s wedge should flex by country: productivity in Singapore, bilingual knowledge in Hong Kong, grassroots team adoption in SEA, and engineering workflows in India.
2. Governance matters everywhere.
Atlassian’s enterprise strength shows that bottom-up adoption only scales when IT trusts the platform. Across all territories, Notion needs to lead with security, compliance, admin controls, data privacy, and enterprise-grade governance.
3. PLG must convert into sales-led expansion.
Slack, Asana, and Monday all show that self-serve adoption is powerful, but enterprise growth comes from converting usage signals into larger, governed deployments. Notion should use PQLs, workspace sprawl, AI usage, and Confluence replacement signals to trigger sales engagement.
4. Ecosystems accelerate trust and scale.
Atlassian built strength through partners and developers; Slack and Asana grew through communities and integrations; Monday scaled with solution-oriented use cases. For APAC, Notion should lean into VCs, SIs, government programs, creators, and local communities to build trust and pipeline.
