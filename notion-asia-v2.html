Execution Rhythms
⚡️ Accountability & Clarity
  • Measure what Matters: For AI GTM Builders, incorporate customer outcomes as key KPI and normalize periodically to build peer ROI.
  • Operational Cadence: Weekly Ownership (Forecasting, Top Deals), MEDDPIC Clinics, Stand-up Scrums on Problems to Solve → Solutioning.

⚠️ Risks & Strategic Asks

1. Capture Speed: Enterprise Conversion Window
Risk: There is a critical window to become the default enterprise AI operating system before legacy vendors or point solutions entrench. Notion requires clear localized use-cases for CXO relevance.

The Ask: Dedicated Marketing, CS, and Solutions Engineering support for:
  • Co-created industry solution demos (Fintech, SaaS, E-Commerce, HealthTech, HRTech).
  • Localized event budget for executive roundtables, webinars, and technical workshops.
  • Customer success spotlights and partner co-selling enablement.
2. Market Dilution: Sequenced Regional Focus
Risk: Overextending coverage across heterogeneous SEA markets risks operational dilution.

The Ask & Alignment: Strict prioritization sequence:
  • Tier 1: Singapore & Hong Kong • High-fit enterprise proof anchors.
  • Tier 2: Malaysia & Philippines • Low-cost emerging market expansion.
  • Tier 3: Indonesia, Thailand & Vietnam • High-upside emerging bets via ecosystem partners.

Roadmap

  • Sharpen Focus: Anchor GTM in SG and HK; prioritize top 6 high-fit industry clusters.
  • Build Operating Model: Lock Top 50 target accounts, PLS triggers, and security qualification maps.

MONTHS 1 – 6
2. Land
  • Win Initial Proof Points: Outbound motion targeting high-fit accounts with existing bottom-up usage.

MONTHS 6 – 12

  • Repeatable Plays: Leverage early wins to refine industry ROI templates and proof cases.

MONTHS 12 – 18
4. Scale
  • Replicate Playbook: Roll the validated SG/HK model into priority SEA and India scale-ups.
  • Ecosystem Leverage: Scale government grant pathways, creator networks, and SI channels.
  • Platform Standard: Cement Notion as the default APAC AI enterprise operating layer.
